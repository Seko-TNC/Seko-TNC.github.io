Update shelf prices without replacing paper labels

Electronic shelf labels are small screens that display prices beside your products. RafTek sends new prices wirelessly to the right labels. In a live demo, you can watch a price change on a real shelf label.

How a new price reaches the shelf

Imagine putting a snack on promotion. Here’s what happens to its shelf price.

  1. Change the price

    Update the price in your store software if it’s connected, or share a price file using the method we agree with you.

  2. Send the update

    RafTek sends the price wirelessly to the shelf label linked to that product.

  3. See it on the shelf

    The label displays the new price. In a trial, we can compare it with the price at the till.

Store benefits

Spend less time changing labels

When prices change, staff have fewer paper tags to print and replace.

Update promotions in one place

Send changes to the labels for the products included in your promotion.

Reduce the chance of price mismatches

Using the same price source can help keep shelf and till prices in line.

Common questions

Can I keep using my current till or store software?

We check what product and price data your system can share. With Mikro, the connection depends on the version and the access you or your software provider can give. A spreadsheet or other file may be an option.

What does a trial cost?

It depends on how many labels you need, how prices are sent and what on-site setup is needed. We can discuss the scope before you decide whether to start.
