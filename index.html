Update shelf prices without replacing paper labels

Electronic shelf labels are small screens that show prices and product details. RafTek updates them wirelessly, so your team can change prices and run offers without printing new labels.

See it in a free demo, then try it in your store for free.

Request a free demo How it works

How a new price reaches the shelf

We fit RafTek around the way you already run your store.

  1. Look at what you use today

    We review your systems and how your team works. In most cases, RafTek can connect to what you already use.

  2. Choose how prices change

    If your system can share price changes, the labels can update automatically. We can also set up updates to suit your needs.

  3. See the change on the shelf

    RafTek sends new prices, offers and product details wirelessly to the right labels.

Choose the right label for each spot

Labels come in different sizes and models, including freezer models and water-resistant models. We help you choose based on where the label will go and what you want it to show.

Spend less time changing labels

What you can use it for

Day-to-day price changes

Update a single item or send new prices to a whole group of products at once.

Timed promotions

Schedule offers in advance and set when they start and end, including when regular prices return.

End-of-day discounts

Set a time to lower prices on items you want to sell that day or clear from stock.

Update product details

Change names, pack sizes or other information shown on the label.

Common questions

Are the demo and pilot free?

Yes, both are free. In the demo, we show a live price change. If you’d like to try it in your store, we agree what to test in the free pilot.

What should I prepare for the demo?

Tell us how you change prices today and what you’d like to see. We can then tailor the demo to your store.

Can my store stay open during the pilot?

Yes. We plan the setup and testing around your store’s routines so your team can keep serving customers as usual.

Can I keep using my current systems?

In most cases, yes. We check what you use and how it can share prices. If you need a different way to send updates, we can set one up with you.

How do I know which label to choose?

We look at the shelf space, how much information the label needs to show, and whether it will be exposed to cold or water.

Can I set an offer to start and end at specific times?

Yes. You can choose when the offer starts and when the regular price returns.

Evet. Kurulumu ve denemeyi günlük işleyişinize göre planlarız, böylece ekibiniz müşterilere her zamanki gibi hizmet verebilir.

Deneme bittikten sonra ne olur?
