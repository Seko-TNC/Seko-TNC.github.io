Business Case

Why retailers move from paper to electronic shelf labels.

Faster price changes

Update shelf-edge pricing centrally instead of printing and replacing paper labels manually.

Improved pricing accuracy

Reduce the risk of shelf prices becoming out of sync with centrally managed product data.

Lower manual workload

Reduce repetitive printing, sorting, distributing and replacing of paper shelf labels.

More flexible promotions

Support more frequent price and promotion changes without creating the same shelf-edge workload.

Centralised management

Create a consistent update process across departments, stores or locations.

Phased rollout

Validate connectivity, workflows and commercial assumptions through a phased deployment model before wider rollout.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can RafTek integrate ESLs with existing product or pricing systems?

Integration depends on the systems involved, but API, middleware and file-based workflows can all be assessed as part of the solution design.

Do we need to replace an entire store at once?

No. RafTek supports phased deployments, allowing hardware, connectivity and operating processes to be confirmed before wider expansion.

Does RafTek only work with one ESL manufacturer?

No. RafTek uses a supplier-flexible approach and selects hardware and integration options according to project requirements, compatibility and commercial fit.

Which markets do you support?

RafTek currently focuses on projects and partnerships across the UK, European Union and Turkey.
