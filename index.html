Update shelf prices without replacing paper labels

Electronic shelf labels are small screens that display prices and product information beside your goods. RafTek updates them wirelessly, so your team can change prices and run promotions without reprinting labels. See a price change on a real shelf label in a live demo.

Request a live demo How it works

How a new price reaches the shelf

We adapt RafTek to how you manage prices, promotions and product information today.

  1. Review your current setup

    We look at your systems and how your team works. In most cases, we can integrate RafTek into the setup you already use.

  2. Choose how updates work

    Where your setup supports it, labels can update automatically when a price changes in your existing system. We can also tailor how and when updates are sent to suit the way you work.

  3. Send updates to the shelf

    RafTek wirelessly sends new prices, promotions or product details to the labels linked to the selected products.

Store benefits

Spend less time changing labels

When prices change, staff have fewer paper tags to print and replace.

Reduce the chance of price mismatches

Using the same price source can help keep shelf and till prices in line.

Roll out changes faster

Send a change to a group of products in one go, from a small group of items to changes across the store.

Enter prices once

When your pricing system is connected, a change there can reach the shelf without your team typing it in again.

Keep control of timing

Choose when scheduled prices take effect and which products are included.

Use less paper

Digital updates reduce how often you need to print and throw away paper labels.

What you can use it for

Day-to-day price changes

Update a single item or send new prices to a whole group of products at once.

Timed promotions

Schedule offers in advance and set when they start and end, including when regular prices return.

Time-sensitive markdowns

Schedule reductions for clearance items or products that need to sell by the end of the day.

Product information updates

Update details such as product names or pack sizes when your range changes, using a label layout that suits your store.

Yeni fiyat rafa nasıl ulaşır?

RafTek’i fiyatları, kampanyaları ve ürün bilgilerini bugün nasıl yönettiğinize göre yapılandırıyoruz.

  1. Mevcut işleyişi inceleyelim

    Önce kullandığınız sistemleri ve ekibinizin çalışma şeklini inceliyoruz. Çoğu durumda RafTek’i mevcut altyapınıza entegre edebiliyoruz.

  2. Güncelleme yöntemini seçin

    Mevcut sisteminiz destekliyorsa, sistemdeki fiyat değiştiğinde raf etiketleri otomatik olarak güncellenebilir. Güncellemelerin nasıl ve ne zaman gönderileceğini işleyişinize göre de yapılandırabiliriz.

  3. Güncellemeleri rafa gönderin

    RafTek, yeni fiyatları, kampanyaları ve ürün bilgilerini seçilen ürünlerin etiketlerine kablosuz olarak gönderir.
